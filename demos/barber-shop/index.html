    Questions

    The short answers.

    Do I need to book?

    No. Walk in if a chair is free. Booking guarantees your time and takes less than a minute.

    Why is there a $10 deposit?

    A no-show is a chair nobody else could take. The deposit comes off your bill on the day, and it is refunded in full if you cancel more than 24 hours ahead. Regulars with a clean record skip it.

    How do I change or cancel?

    Use the link in your confirmation or reminder text. More than 24 hours out: full refund. Between 2 and 24 hours: the deposit stays as credit for next time. Under 2 hours or a no-show: the deposit is kept.

    A time I want is full. What now?

    Join the waitlist for that day. If someone cancels you get a text, and the first person to tap gets the chair.

    How do I pay for the cut itself?

    Card or cash at the chair. Only the deposit is taken online.
