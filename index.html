How we work this out
  • No-shows: bookings a week × 52 ÷ 12 × the share missed × the value of a booking. Reminders alone cut missed appointments by roughly 29% in a review of 29 studies (systematic review of SMS and phone reminders). Deposits and a waitlist that refills cancellations should take it further, so we use 30%, 45% and 60% for cautious, realistic and optimistic.
  • Enquiries: enquiries × the share that go cold × 65% who then try someone else × your close rate × a new customer's value. The 65% is our assumption, published surveys vary widely. Speed matters: in a study of online sales leads, companies that responded within an hour were about seven times as likely to qualify the lead as those that took longer (Harvard Business Review, 2011). We win back 25%, 45% or 65% of what is lost.
  • Admin: hours × 52 ÷ 12 × what your hour is worth. We assume automation gives back 30%, 50% or 70% of it.
  • Not counted: repeat visits, reviews and word of mouth. Those are real, but harder to put a number on, so this probably understates things.

An illustration built from your numbers and published averages, not a forecast. We do not guarantee a figure, and we will tell you honestly if we think automation will not pay for itself in your business.

    Questions

    The questions we get every time.

    How long does it take?

    Weeks, not months. AI does the heavy lifting on the build, so a website on its own is usually live inside two to three weeks, and Flow, with booking and automations, inside four to six. Custom builds are timed on your quote. We give you a date before we start and we hold to it.

    What if my business does not work like a clinic?

    Good. The example on this page tracks people and appointments because that is who we built it for. Your business might track stock, class passes, consent forms or parts. Same engine, built around your work. You do not bend to ours.

    Do I own it, or am I renting it?

    You own it. The monthly fee covers hosting, maintenance and the features in your package, not a licence to keep using your own system. Your data leaves with you any time, whole and usable.

    What happens after it launches?

    We keep looking after it. Hosting, updates, and a direct line to the person who built it. Flow and custom builds include ongoing tuning as your business changes.

    Can I start small and add to it later?

    Most people do. Start with Flow, or with the few pieces you pick in a custom quote, and add the AI front desk, phone answering or integrations later. Adding something is a change to what we run for you, not a rebuild from scratch.

    I do not know what I need. Where do I start?

    You do not have to know. Use the calculator to see where time and money are slipping, pick a goal in the quote studio and we suggest a starting list, or just tell us how you work and we will build the list with you. Nothing is a commitment until you say so.

    Is the custom price final?

    The quote studio gives you an honest figure from our published prices. Anything that is not on the list, like stock, class passes, consent forms or parts, is quoted after a short call, and the final price is confirmed in writing before any work starts.

    Why is the price on the website?

    Because you should be able to work out whether we are worth a call without booking one. The price you see is the price we quote, and we tell you before any work starts.
